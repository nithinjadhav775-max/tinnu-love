A Letter For You 💌

Things I don't always know how to say

Dear Tinnu,
I don't know exactly when you became so important to me. Maybe it happened slowly, through the little conversations, the smiles, the memories and all those tiny moments that somehow stayed in my heart.

When I look at these pictures, I don't just see photographs. I see moments. I see a person who makes ordinary memories feel special.

I may not always have the perfect words, but one thing is simple — having you in my life means more to me than I can properly explain.

So I made this little website just for you. Not because a website can explain everything I feel, but because I wanted to create one small place where our memories could stay.

If you ever forget how special you are to me, come back here. ❤️

— Nithin
