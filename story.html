Dear Naina... ❤️

You know what's funny?

The first time I saw you, I didn't know that one day you would become someone so special to me.

I just knew there was something about you.

Maybe it was your eyes.

And honestly, your name makes perfect sense — Naina means eyes. ❤️

Your eyes were one of the first things I noticed about you… and somehow, they're still one of the things I can't stop looking at.

But it's not just your eyes.

You're beautiful in so many little ways — your smile, your expressions, the way you talk, and especially the way you can somehow keep talking forever. 😂❤️

Yes, you talk A LOT.

But here's the funny part… I actually love listening to you.

I could probably listen to you talk about absolutely nothing for hours and still somehow enjoy it.

And then there's us... ❤️

Those classroom days.

Passing little notes to each other when we were supposed to be paying attention. 😂

Trying to act completely normal when we both knew exactly what we were doing.

Those tiny pieces of paper somehow became our little secret world.

And remember all those stupid conversations and plans?

Even the ones where we thought about finally getting that kiss… and then somehow, every single time, we never actually did it. 😂❤️

We had the plan.
We had the courage for approximately five seconds.
And then...
Nothing. 😂

But honestly?

Those awkward, silly, crazy little moments are some of my favorite memories.

Because they were ours.

Nobody else knew what was going on inside those little letters.

Nobody else knew why we were smiling.

Nobody else knew what those tiny pieces of paper meant to us.

And somewhere between those notes, those endless conversations, your beautiful eyes, your smile, your craziness, and all the moments we probably shouldn't have been having in class...

I fell for you. ❤️

I didn't plan it.
I didn't expect it.
It just happened.

And now when I think about beautiful things, somehow my mind always finds its way back to you.

So Naina, if you ever look at yourself and wonder whether you're beautiful...

I hope you remember this:

There is someone who looks at you and sees more than just a beautiful girl.

He sees the girl who made ordinary classroom days unforgettable.

The girl whose letters were worth hiding.

The girl whose endless talking somehow became his favorite sound.

The girl with those beautiful eyes.

The girl who made him fall in love without even trying.

That girl is you. ❤️

And if I could go back to the very first day I saw you...

I'd still look at you.
I'd still smile.
I'd still pass you that first little letter.

And I'd still fall for you all over again.

Every single time. ❤️

— Papa ❤️
